About the club

Two friends, one oven

The Bread Club is Eliana and Shayna, two friends baking sourdough in Ra'anana.

Last year we baked plain loaves for neighbours and friends. This year we came back with new flavors and new packaging, and the same slow sourdough.

Every loaf is baked to order, twice a week.

Questions

Can I order a different flour, like spelt?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp, minimum 2 loaves.

Do you deliver outside Ra'anana?

We deliver around Ra'anana, Herzliya and close by. For bigger orders we're happy to drive further, so message us first.

I have an allergy.

Please write it in the notes when you order.

How do you match my Bit payment?

By your full name in the Bit note, so please don't skip it.
